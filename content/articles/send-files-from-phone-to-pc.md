**To move files from a phone to a computer without a cable, open Relay on both, connect them, and send.** The files go straight across your Wi-Fi at full quality, and on Chrome or Edge the PC can save big files directly to a folder.

## Steps

1. On the **computer**, open [relay.rishishah.in](/) in Chrome or Edge and click **Start sending**.
2. On the **phone**, scan the QR code with the camera. On the same Wi-Fi you can also tap the computer under **Nearby**.
3. On the computer, click **Allow** for the phone.
4. On the phone, tap the upload area and choose photos, videos or files.
5. On the computer, click **Save as…** to stream each file straight into a folder, or **Accept** and then **Save**.

## Going the other way (PC → phone)

Same steps: drop files on the computer and accept them on the phone. Paste a screenshot on the PC with Ctrl+V and it's on your phone a second later.

## Tips for big videos

- Plug the phone in and keep the screen on.
- Use **Save as…** on the PC so the file never sits in browser memory.
- If Wi-Fi drops, Relay picks up where it left off. See [resuming transfers](/resume-interrupted-file-transfer).

## FAQ

### How do I transfer files from Android to PC without USB?
Open Relay on both devices, connect with the QR code or Nearby, and send. No app or cable needed.

### How do I get photos from my iPhone to a Windows PC?
Open Relay in Safari on the iPhone and in Chrome or Edge on the PC, connect, and send. The originals arrive at full quality.

### Is it faster than uploading to Google Drive?
On the same Wi-Fi, yes. The file only travels across your local network once.
