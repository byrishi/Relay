**AirDrop is built for Apple devices. Google's Quick Share can now exchange files with AirDrop on some Android phones (starting with the Pixel 10 in late 2025), but Windows, Linux and most other combinations still need a cross-platform tool.** Relay works in any browser with no install; LocalSend is a native app for your local network.

## Options at a glance

| Tool | iPhone ↔ Android | iPhone ↔ Windows | Install | Works across networks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Relay** | ✓ | ✓ | No (browser) | ✓ |
| **LocalSend** | ✓ | ✓ | App on each device | Same network only |
| **PairDrop** | ✓ | ✓ | No (browser) | ✓ (rooms/pairing) |
| **Quick Share** | Some phones (via AirDrop, Pixel 10 first) | Android ↔ Windows only | Built into Android, app on Windows | Nearby only |
| **AirDrop** | Only with supported Quick Share phones | ✗ | Built in | Nearby only |

## The AirDrop-like way with Relay

1. Open [relay.rishishah.in](/) on both devices, on the same Wi-Fi.
2. Each device appears under **Nearby** on the other. Tap it.
3. Accept the invitation on the other device, then send. It works with photos, videos, documents and whole folders.

Not on the same network? Press **Start Transfer** and share the code or QR instead.

Install Relay from the browser menu to open it like an app. On Android you can then pick **Share to Relay** from any app's share menu.

## Why Relay is a good AirDrop substitute

- **No size limit**, and big files stream straight to disk on desktop Chrome and Edge.
- **Private:** end-to-end encrypted, nothing uploaded, a [safety number](/verify-safety-number) to verify.
- **Works anywhere:** different networks, different operating systems.

## FAQ

### Is there AirDrop for Windows?
Not from Apple. Relay works between an iPhone and Windows in the browser with no install; LocalSend does it with an app on both.

### How do I AirDrop from iPhone to Android?
On a Pixel 10 or another phone with Quick Share–AirDrop support, use AirDrop directly. For every other Android phone, open Relay in Safari on the iPhone and Chrome on the Android phone, connect with Nearby or a code, and send. [Step-by-step guide](/send-files-from-iphone-to-android).

### Is there an AirDrop alternative that works without Wi-Fi?
Relay needs an internet connection to introduce the devices. LocalSend works on a local network without internet.
