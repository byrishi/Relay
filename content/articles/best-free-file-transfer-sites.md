**The best free file transfer site depends on one thing: will the other person be online when you send?** If yes, a direct peer-to-peer tool such as Relay has no size limit and never stores your file. If they will download later, you need an upload service, and every free one caps size, count or how long the link lives.

## Which one to pick

- **Sending to someone right now** (a colleague, your own laptop, a friend on a call): use a direct tool. [Relay](/) streams the file straight to their browser, encrypted end to end, with no size limit and no account. See [how direct transfer works](/send-files-without-uploading).
- **They will download it tomorrow:** use an upload service. Smash and Filemail are generous for single large files; WeTransfer's free plan now runs out after 3 GB a month.
- **It's sensitive** (contracts, IDs, medical files): prefer end-to-end encryption, so the service itself can't read the file. Relay and Wormhole both do this; WeTransfer, Smash and Google Drive do not. See [secure file sharing](/secure-file-sharing).
- **It's a whole folder:** Relay keeps the folder structure, or you can zip it first for upload services. See [how to send a folder](/send-a-folder-online).

## Why direct transfer is often the better free option

Upload services pay to store your file, so their free plans have to be limited. A direct transfer stores nothing: the file goes from your device to theirs, so there is nothing to cap, nothing to expire and nothing left on a server afterwards. The trade-off is that both devices must be online at the same time.

On most home and mobile networks the two devices connect directly. Some strict office or campus networks block that, and then a relay server is needed to pass the encrypted data along.

## FAQ

### What is the best free file transfer site with no size limit?
If both devices can be online together, a peer-to-peer tool like Relay has no size limit because nothing is uploaded. Every free upload service caps size: Smash queues files over 2 GB, Filemail stops at 5 GB per transfer, and WeTransfer allows 3 GB per 30 days.

### Is free file transfer safe?
It depends on who can read the file. Services that upload your file hold the encryption keys and can access it. End-to-end encrypted tools such as Relay and Wormhole keep the keys on your devices only.

### Do I need an account to transfer files for free?
Not with Relay, Smash, Filemail or Wormhole. Google Drive needs a Google account. See [sending files without signing up](/send-files-without-signup).
