**The quickest way to send files between an iPhone and an Android phone, with no app and no quality loss, is to open Relay in both browsers and send directly.** Relay sends exactly the file you pick, byte for byte, with no extra compression.

## Steps

1. On **both phones**, open [relay.rishishah.in](/) (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android).
2. **Same Wi-Fi?** Each phone appears under **Nearby** on the other. Tap it and accept the invitation.
   **Different networks?** Tap **Start sending** on one phone and type the code (or scan the QR) on the other.
3. Allow the device, then tap the upload area and choose your photos, videos or files.
4. On the receiving phone, tap **Accept**, then **Save** when it finishes.

Works the same in the other direction, Android to iPhone.

## Tips

- **Keep both screens on** until the transfer finishes; phones pause background tabs.
- **Want the untouched originals?** When you pick from the photo library, iOS can convert HEIC photos to JPEG and re-encode videos. To send the exact originals, save them to the **Files** app first and pick them from there.
- **Lots of photos?** Select many at once. Each arrives as a separate file.

## FAQ

### Can I AirDrop from iPhone to Android?
Only to Android phones that support Quick Share's AirDrop interoperability, starting with the Pixel 10. For other phones, use a browser tool like Relay.

### Will photos lose quality?
Relay never compresses anything: the exact file you pick is sent byte for byte. (iOS itself may convert photos picked from the library; pick from the Files app to avoid that.)

### Do I need to install an app?
No. Relay runs in Safari and Chrome.
