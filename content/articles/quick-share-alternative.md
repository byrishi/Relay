**Quick Share is great between Android phones, but it doesn't reach Macs or Linux, and only some Android phones can send to an iPhone.** For any mix of devices, use a browser-based tool like Relay: open it on both devices, scan a QR code, and send. Nothing to install, no size limit.

## Where Quick Share works, and where it doesn't

- **Android to Android, Chromebook:** built in, fast, works offline.
- **Windows:** with Google's Quick Share app.
- **iPhone:** only from phones that support AirDrop sharing. As of mid-2026 that's the Pixel 10 and 9 series, Pixel 9a and 8a, Samsung Galaxy S24, S25 and S26 and the recent Fold and Flip models on One UI 8.5, plus a few phones from Xiaomi, OnePlus, Oppo, Vivo and Honor. Both sides must be set to receive from **Everyone**.
- **Mac and Linux:** not supported.

## Alternatives for every combination

| You need | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Android ↔ Mac | Relay | Browser on both, no app |
| Android ↔ iPhone (older phones) | Relay or LocalSend | Works on any model |
| Anything ↔ Linux | Relay or LocalSend | Quick Share has no Linux app |
| A work PC where you can't install apps | Relay | Runs in the browser |
| No internet at all | LocalSend | Works over the local network only |

## Send from Android to a Mac with Relay

1. On the Mac, open [relay.rishishah.in](/) and press **Start sending**.
2. On the phone, scan the QR code with the camera.
3. Approve the phone, pick the files, and accept them on the Mac.

On the same Wi-Fi you can skip the QR code: the other device appears under **Nearby**. See [sharing on the same Wi-Fi](/share-files-on-same-wifi).

## FAQ

### Can Quick Share send to an iPhone?
Only from Android phones that support AirDrop sharing, such as recent Pixels and Galaxy S24 and later on One UI 8.5, and only when both devices accept from Everyone. Relay works with any iPhone through the browser.

### Is there Quick Share for Mac?
No. Use a browser tool like Relay or an app like LocalSend.

### Does Relay work without internet?
No. Relay needs a connection so the two devices can find each other; the file itself then goes directly between them. For fully offline sharing, use Quick Share or LocalSend.
