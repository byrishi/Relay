**You can send files with no account at all.** With Relay you open a page, connect the other device with a code or QR, and send: no sign-up, no email address, no app. Some upload sites such as Smash and Filemail also skip registration for small transfers, but they keep your file on their servers for days.

## Why skip the account

- **Less personal data.** No email address, password or contact list handed over.
- **Faster.** No verification emails before your first transfer.
- **Works on borrowed devices.** A shared office PC or a friend's laptop, without logging in to anything you have to remember to log out of.

## Options that don't ask you to register

| Tool | What it asks for | Where your file goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Relay** | Nothing | Straight to the other device, never stored |
| Smash | Nothing required; an email address is optional | Its servers, kept 7 days |
| Filemail | Nothing for free transfers | Its servers, kept 7 days |
| Wormhole | Nothing | Its servers (encrypted), 24 hours |
| LocalSend | Nothing, but an app on both devices | Straight to the other device, same Wi-Fi |

## How Relay works without an account

Accounts exist so a service can remember who you are between visits. Relay doesn't need to: it only connects two devices while both pages are open.

1. Open [Relay](/) and press **Start sending**. You get a random room code like `satya-ganga-108`, a link and a QR code.
2. The other person opens the link. You see their device and approve it.
3. Files go directly between you, encrypted end to end. Close the tab and the room is gone.

Each device gets a random name, and both screens show a [safety number](/verify-safety-number) you can compare to be sure you're connected to the right person.

## Is "no account" the same as anonymous?

Not quite. Without an account nobody holds a profile on you, but a direct connection lets the other device see your IP address, just like a video call. Use a VPN if that matters. See [private file transfer](/private-file-transfer).

## FAQ

### How can I send a file without creating an account?
Open Relay on both devices and connect them with the room code or QR. No sign-up or email is needed.

### Is there a file size limit without an account?
Not with Relay, because nothing is uploaded. Upload sites keep limits even without registration, for example 5 GB per transfer on Filemail.

### Can I send files anonymously?
Relay doesn't collect names, emails or files. The other device can see your IP address during the transfer, so use a VPN if you need to hide it.
