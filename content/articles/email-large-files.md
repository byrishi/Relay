**You can't attach a file bigger than about 25 MB to an email, so send a link instead.** Either share it from cloud storage (Gmail and Outlook do this for you), put it on a transfer site, or skip email and send it directly to the other person's device with a tool like Relay, which has no size limit.

## Why the email says the file is too big

| Provider | Largest email you can send |
|---|---|
| Gmail | 25 MB |
| Outlook.com | 20 MB |
| Yahoo Mail | 25 MB |
| iCloud Mail | 20 MB (Mail Drop handles larger files) |

Email turns attachments into text before sending, which makes them about a third bigger. So a 20 MB PDF can already fail on a 25 MB limit. See all [file size limits](/file-size-limits).

## Option 1: let your email app make a cloud link

- **Gmail:** attach the file as usual. Above 25 MB Gmail uploads it to Google Drive and inserts a link. Check the sharing setting so the recipient can open it.
- **Outlook:** choose **Share link** for OneDrive instead of attaching a copy.
- **Apple Mail:** turn on **Mail Drop** and large attachments are sent as an iCloud link that lasts 30 days.

Good for: people who expect email. Costs: your cloud storage, and the file sits on a server until you delete it.

## Option 2: a file transfer site

Upload the file to a site such as WeTransfer, Smash or Filemail and paste the link into your email. Free plans have limits: WeTransfer gives 3 GB per 30 days, Filemail 5 GB per transfer, and Smash queues anything over 2 GB. Compare them in [the best free file transfer sites](/best-free-file-transfer-sites).

## Option 3: send it directly, no upload

If the other person can open a link while you are online, there is no reason to upload at all.

1. Open [Relay](/) and drop the file on the page.
2. Copy the link it gives you and paste it into your email or chat.
3. When they open it, approve their device and the file goes straight to them, encrypted end to end.

Nothing is stored, so there is no size limit, no expiry and nothing to clean up afterwards.

## Option 4: compress it (only sometimes)

Zipping helps documents, spreadsheets and folders of text files. Photos, videos and PDFs full of images are already compressed and barely shrink, so a ZIP rarely gets a 200 MB video under 25 MB.

## FAQ

### What is the maximum attachment size for Gmail?
25 MB per email you send. Anything larger is sent as a Google Drive link.

### How do I send a 1 GB file by email?
Don't attach it. Share a cloud link, use a transfer site, or send a Relay link: the recipient opens it and the file streams straight to their device.

### Is it safe to email large files through a link?
Anyone with the link can usually open it. For private files, restrict the link to the recipient's email address, or use an end-to-end encrypted tool. See [secure file sharing](/secure-file-sharing).
