**Send the video as a document, not from the gallery.** WhatsApp compresses gallery videos to fit 16 MB, usually at 720p or 480p. A video sent as a document arrives untouched, up to 2 GB. For anything bigger, send it directly with a tool like Relay, which has no size limit.

## Why WhatsApp ruins video quality

Videos picked from the gallery or camera are re-encoded to stay under 16 MB. Long or 4K clips are cut down hard: lower resolution, lower bitrate, blocky motion. Photos are scaled to about 1600 pixels. Documents are left alone.

## Send a video as a document (up to 2 GB)

**On Android**
1. Open the chat and tap the paperclip.
2. Choose **Document**, then **Browse other docs**.
3. Find the video (usually in DCIM › Camera) and send.

**On iPhone**
1. Save the video to the Files app first (in Photos: Share › Save to Files).
2. In the chat, tap **+** › **Document** and pick it from Files.

The recipient sees a file card instead of a preview and has to download it before playing, but it plays at full quality.

## Bigger than 2 GB, or a whole shoot?

Long 4K recordings pass 2 GB quickly. Send them directly instead:

1. Open [Relay](/) on your phone and on the other device.
2. Connect with the QR code, a room code, or tap the device if you are on the same Wi-Fi.
3. Choose the videos. They go straight across in original quality, with no size limit and no upload.

If the connection drops, the transfer [resumes from where it stopped](/resume-interrupted-file-transfer).

## FAQ

### What is the WhatsApp video size limit?
16 MB for videos sent normally, which WhatsApp compresses to fit. Up to 2 GB when sent as a document.

### Does sending as a document keep the original quality?
Yes. Documents are not compressed, so resolution, bitrate and metadata stay as they were.

### How do I send a 5 GB video from my phone?
Use a direct transfer such as Relay, which has no size limit, or upload it to cloud storage and share the link. See [sending large video files](/send-large-video-files).
