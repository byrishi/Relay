**SHAREit and Xender were among the 59 apps India blocked in June 2020, and the ban has not been lifted.** Good replacements: Quick Share for Android to Android or Windows, Relay for any device with a browser (iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac, Linux), and LocalSend if you prefer an app on the same Wi-Fi. All three are free and ad-free.

## Why people are moving away from SHAREit and Xender

- **Blocked in India.** Both are on the government's June 2020 list of 59 apps, along with TikTok and UC Browser. They are not on the Play Store or App Store in India, so the only copies around are old installs or APKs from unofficial sites, which is how malware spreads.
- **Ads and bundled installs.** Both apps were known for full-screen ads and prompts to install other apps.
- **Security concerns.** India's defence ministry had already asked the armed forces to remove SHAREit in 2017 over data concerns.

### Relay: no app, any device

[Relay](/) runs in the browser, so it works the same between an iPhone and a Windows laptop, or an Android phone and a Mac. Open it on both devices, scan the QR code, and send. Files go device to device, encrypted end to end, with no size limit and no ads. On the same Wi-Fi the other device shows up on its own. See [phone to PC](/send-files-from-phone-to-pc).

### Quick Share: best for Android to Android

Built into Android (it replaced Nearby Share). Fast and offline, but mostly limited to Android, Chromebooks and Windows with Google's app. Some recent phones can now share with iPhones over AirDrop. See [Quick Share alternatives](/quick-share-alternative).

### LocalSend: an open-source app for the same Wi-Fi

Free and open source, no account, and works without internet as long as both devices are on the same network. You do need to install it on both. [Relay vs LocalSend](/relay-vs-localsend).

## How to send files from phone to laptop without SHAREit

1. On the laptop, open [relay.rishishah.in](/) and press **Start sending**.
2. On the phone, open the camera and scan the QR code.
3. Approve the phone and pick the photos, videos or documents. They arrive at full quality.

## FAQ

### Is SHAREit banned in India?
Yes. SHAREit and Xender were blocked in June 2020 as part of a list of 59 apps and remain blocked as of 2026.

### What is the safest SHAREit alternative?
One that's open source and needs no sketchy APK. Relay and LocalSend are open source; Quick Share comes from Google and is built into Android.

### Is there a SHAREit alternative for PC?
Yes. Relay works in any browser on Windows, Mac or Linux with nothing to install. LocalSend and Quick Share for Windows are apps.

### Which alternative works between iPhone and Android?
Relay and LocalSend both work across iPhone and Android. Quick Share works with iPhones only on some newer Android phones.
