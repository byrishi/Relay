**To send a large video at full quality, transfer the original file directly to the other device instead of through a messaging app or a capped cloud service.** With Relay there's no size limit and no compression; the video arrives byte for byte.

## Why videos lose quality or fail

- **Messaging apps** (WhatsApp, Messenger and others) usually re-encode videos to save bandwidth.
- **Email** caps attachments at around 20 to 25 MB.
- **Free cloud transfer plans** cap size: WeTransfer free allows 3 GB per 30 days.

## Send it directly

1. Open [Relay](/) on both devices and connect them (Nearby, code or QR).
2. Drop the video. On a desktop receiver, use **Save as…** in Chrome or Edge so it streams straight to disk.
3. Keep both devices awake until it completes.

## Tips for multi-gigabyte footage

- **Same network = fastest.** A 20 GB file over good Wi-Fi or Ethernet moves at local speed, not internet speed.
- **Send the folder** of clips in one go; structure is kept. [How to send a folder](/send-a-folder-online).
- **Don't worry about drops.** Relay [resumes](/resume-interrupted-file-transfer) from the last byte.
- **From iPhone:** pick videos from the Files app to avoid iOS re-encoding them.

## FAQ

### How do I send a 10 GB video for free?
Use a direct transfer tool like Relay. There's no limit and no upload; keep both devices online until it finishes.

### Does Relay compress videos?
No. Relay sends the exact file. Any conversion would come from the device's own picker (for example iOS converting from the photo library).

### What's the fastest way to send a big video to someone nearby?
Open Relay on the same Wi-Fi and use Nearby. The video moves at local network speed.
