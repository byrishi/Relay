**If WeTransfer's free plan (3 GB per 30 days, links expire after 3 days) is holding you back, the best free alternative depends on one question: will the recipient be online at the same time as you?** If yes, send it directly with a peer-to-peer tool like Relay. If not, use an encrypted link service or a cloud drive.

## When you're both online: direct transfer

**Relay** streams the file straight to the other device in the browser.

- No size limit and no monthly cap
- End-to-end encrypted, with a [safety number](/verify-safety-number) to verify it
- Nothing stored, nothing to expire or delete
- Works on any device, no account

Other good direct options: **PairDrop** (browser) and **LocalSend** (app, same network only). See [best P2P file sharing apps](/best-p2p-file-sharing-apps).

## When the recipient downloads later

- **Wormhole** (wormhole.app): end-to-end encrypted in the browser; files up to 5 GB are stored for 24 hours, larger files go peer-to-peer while your page stays open. [Relay vs Wormhole](/relay-vs-wormhole).
- **Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox:** share a link from your existing storage; limited by your quota (Google gives 15 GB free).

## Quick picker

| Situation | Use |
|---|---|
| Huge file, recipient online now | Relay |
| Private file, want zero copies on servers | Relay |
| Recipient downloads tomorrow, under 5 GB | Wormhole |
| File already in your cloud drive | Drive / OneDrive / Dropbox link |
| Same Wi-Fi, native app preferred | LocalSend |

## FAQ

### What is the best free alternative to WeTransfer?
For large or private files when both people are online, Relay: no size limit, no account, end-to-end encrypted. If the recipient downloads later, Wormhole or a cloud drive link.

### Is there a WeTransfer alternative with no file size limit?
Yes. Direct peer-to-peer tools like Relay have no limit because the file is never stored on a server.

### Do WeTransfer alternatives require an account?
Relay, PairDrop and Wormhole don't. Cloud drives do.
