**The most secure way to move files between your own devices is a direct, end-to-end encrypted transfer that never stores a copy in the cloud.** With Relay that takes three taps and nothing to install.

## Steps

1. Open [Relay](/) on both devices.
2. On the same network, tap the other device under **Nearby**; elsewhere, use the code or QR.
3. For anything sensitive, open the device name and [check the safety number](/verify-safety-number) matches on both screens.
4. Send. On the receiving computer choose **Save as…** to write straight to disk.

## Why this is safer than syncing through the cloud

- **No third-party copy:** passwords exports, ID scans, tax documents and private photos never land on a server.
- **Verified link:** the safety number proves the connection goes straight between your two devices.
- **Only what you approve:** your devices must accept each file, so nothing arrives unexpectedly.

## Good habits

- Delete sensitive files from the source device after transfer if you no longer need them there.
- Turn off **Keep history** on shared computers.
- Use Relay's **clipboard sync** only between your own approved devices, and switch it off when done.

## FAQ

### What is the safest way to transfer files from phone to laptop?
A direct, end-to-end encrypted transfer such as Relay over your own Wi-Fi, with the safety number verified. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

### Is Bluetooth file transfer secure?
Modern Bluetooth pairing is encrypted but slow and range-limited. A local Wi-Fi transfer with Relay is much faster and verifiable.
