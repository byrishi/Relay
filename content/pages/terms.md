## 2. The service

Relay is a browser-based tool that connects two devices so they can send files to each other directly over WebRTC. Our servers only help the devices find each other; file contents travel between the devices and are not stored by us.

We do not monitor, review or endorse what people transfer, and we cannot see file contents.
